Different Wrinkles Need Different Treatments

Lines caused by repeated facial movement, volume change, laxity, and surface texture do not respond identically.

ResurFX™ is generally best positioned for skin-quality and textural lines; your consultation determines whether laser treatment, injectables, skincare, or a combination better matches your goals.

Collagen Remodeling for Gradual Skin Renewal

Controlled fractional energy reaches the dermis without removing the outer layer. The skin’s repair response supports new collagen formation, which can improve smoothness and the visible quality of fine lines over time.

Who May Be a Candidate?

A consultation is required. You may be considered if:

  • You are concerned about fine textural lines or overall skin quality
  • You prefer progressive improvement rather than a dramatic one-session change
  • You understand laser resurfacing does not replace every wrinkle treatment
  • You can follow sun avoidance and aftercare guidance
Clinical note: Treatment may be postponed or changed based on pregnancy status, medications, active infection or inflammation, recent tanning, photosensitivity, prior pigment reactions, or other medical factors. The treating clinician determines candidacy.
